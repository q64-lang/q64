q64 audio-worklet — a live q64 voice

Unlike audio-web's offline renders, this page runs one persistent q64 wasm instance on the audio thread: an AudioWorklet calls its process() export every 128-frame quantum (~2 µs of the 2 667 µs budget in a debug build). Oscillator phase and filter state live in guest buffers across calls, and slider moves are smoothed inside the q64 code, so they are click-free while the sound never stops.
